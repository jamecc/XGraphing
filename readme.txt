XGraphing - README

Welcome to XGraphing! This software is designed to help you create a variety of visual charts to enhance your workflow, including Mind Maps, Organizational Charts, Gantt Charts, Fishbone Diagrams, and more. With an intuitive, user-friendly interface and powerful features, XGraphing aims to streamline your work processes and improve productivity.

Key Features:
1 Mind Mapping: Easily create detailed and hierarchical mind maps to organize ideas, brainstorm, and plan projects.
2 Organizational Chart: Design clear and structured org charts to represent company or team structures.
3 Gantt Chart: Efficiently manage projects by tracking tasks, timelines, and dependencies with Gantt charts.
4 Fishbone Diagram: Use Fishbone diagrams for root cause analysis and problem-solving.
5 Local Operation: XGraphing runs locally on your computer, ensuring fast performance and offline availability.
6 Data Privacy & Security: Your data stays private and secure, stored locally, with no external data transmission, minimizing privacy risks.

System Requirements:
1 Operating System: Windows 10 or higher
2 Processor: 1.5 GHz or faster processor
3 Memory: 2 GB RAM or more
4 Disk Space: 100 MB of available disk space
5 Display: 1024x768 resolution or higher

Installation Instructions:
1 Download the XGraphing installation package.
2 Double-click the installer to begin the installation process.
3 Follow the on-screen instructions to install XGraphing on your computer.
4 Once installed, you can launch the software from the Start Menu or desktop shortcut.

Data Privacy & Security:
XGraphing runs entirely on your local machine, ensuring your data is kept private.
No data is sent to external servers, protecting your intellectual property and sensitive information.
We take your privacy seriously, and we implement strong security measures to protect your data from any potential leaks.
